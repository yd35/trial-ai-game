World backstory:
The Democracy of Levin has unearthed the ruins of the Concordia civilisation. 
A technologically advanced society who lived long ago. Their technology includes highly intelligent AI, 
able to perform any tasks given to them without fail, or so it would seem. One of these AIs, Viridis, 
is put in charge of Levin’s water system, until a contamination causes an illness. Viridis is swiftly 
prosecuted. Your role as the adjudicator is to decide whether Viridis is guilty or not, with knowledge 
gained from two witnesses (human witness, Seymour and AI witness, ORACLE) and VIRIDIS itself.
